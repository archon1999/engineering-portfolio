# Olympiad organization and competition record

## KEP Junior Algorithmics · 2024

Nazarbek Baltabaev organized the school programming competition through KEP.uz and co-authored the final problem set. The event had two online qualifiers, on **8 and 16 June**, followed by a final at **IT Park, Khorezm, on 7 July 2024**. The final included a programming contest and an Arena format.

The official results report **more than 200 participants** across the month-long event. The initial announcement set a **20,000,000 UZS prize fund**.

## REVO CUP · 2025

Co-organized with **REVO Global** across competitive programming and cybersecurity/CTF. Qualifiers ran on KEP.uz on **29 and 30 March 2025**; the offline final took place at **IT Park, Urgench, on 5 April 2025**, within REVO DAY.

The organizer’s closing report acknowledges Nazarbek’s organizational work and KEP.uz’s contribution. REVO’s official event recap records an **80,000,000 UZS prize fund**.

- [REVO’s official competition announcement and schedule](https://revolution.uz/uz/blog/revo-cup-2025-programming-and-cybersecurity-contest)
- [Organizer’s closing report and personal acknowledgment](https://t.me/dev_story/766)
- [REVO Global’s official recap](https://t.me/RevolutionGlobalLLC/49)

## National online programming competition · 2020

The **State Inspectorate for Supervision of Education Quality** held a national online programming competition. The qualifier was on **26 April 2020** and the final on **9 May 2020**.

In the **student category**, the official result lists **Baltabayev Nazarbek, TUIT Urgench Branch — 2nd place, 7 solved problems**. This is the event behind the previously unlabelled Yandex Contest 18299 result.
