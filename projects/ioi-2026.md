# IOI 2026

**Contestant environments and technical preparation.**

Member of the IOI 2026 Host Technical Committee (HTC), engaged by the Digital Education Development Center on a service contract. The center operates under Uzbekistan’s Ministry of Digital Technologies.

| Organization | Role | Period |

GDB and VS Code debugging configuration and reliability work.

Local resources and environment configuration for the event’s technical workflows.
