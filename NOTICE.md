# Artwork and project context

Project covers are original SVG illustrations. Large text uses outlined Inter and Alpha Lyrae glyphs; the SIL Open Font Licenses are in `assets/licenses/`. Product marks and company names retain their respective owners’ rights.

This repository documents professional contributions and links to available public implementations. It does not contain or grant rights to private company code. Architecture diagrams show high-level product boundaries rather than an exhaustive deployment specification.

The KEP snapshot in `data/platform-snapshot.json` records its public source and observation time. The regularly refreshed version is in the [profile repository](https://github.com/archon1999/archon1999/blob/main/data/products.json). The Cafe Postcode 200+ branch figure describes the broader product’s operating footprint, as reported by its developer.
